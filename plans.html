💰Savings

Endowment Plans

Life cover together with disciplined savings, with a payout when the policy matures.

  • Cover during the term plus a maturity benefit
  • Suits people who like a fixed saving habit
  • Best for: medium-term goals like a home or education

🔁Savings

Money-Back Plans

Receive part of the sum assured at regular intervals while life cover stays in place.

  • Regular payouts during the policy term
  • Cover continues for the full term
  • Best for: those who want cash flow along the way

Whole Life Plans

Cover that continues for a very long period, with benefits that can pass on to your family.

📈Investment

Unit Linked Plans (ULIPs)

Life cover and wealth creation in one plan, with your premium invested in market-linked funds.

  • Choose equity, debt or balanced funds
  • Returns are market-linked and not guaranteed
  • Best for: long-term investors who accept market risk
Ask about ULIPs
📊Investment

Mutual Funds

Grow wealth over time with SIPs or lump-sum investments matched to your goals and risk comfort.

  • Start a SIP with a small monthly amount
  • Returns are market-linked and not guaranteed
  • Best for: long-term goals like retirement or a home
